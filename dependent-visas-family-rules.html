Many people think dependent visa holders can work freely without any restrictions. See dependent visa requirements for details. Dependents cannot work without obtaining permission to engage in other activities, and even with permission, there are hour limits. Some believe that if the primary visa holder is doing well, dependents do not need to worry about their own status. In reality, dependent status has its own requirements and can be revoked independently. Another common belief is that dependent visa holders can study full-time at language schools or universities without changing status. Extended full-time study may require switching to a student visa. Others assume that marriage to the primary visa holder guarantees dependent status will be approved or renewed automatically. Immigration still evaluates whether the relationship is genuine and whether financial support is adequate.

Immigration expects the primary visa holder to financially support their dependents. If a dependent is working excessive hours or the primary holder is not earning enough, immigration may question whether the dependent relationship is genuine. What usually does not matter: dependents taking short part-time jobs, studying Japanese part-time, or traveling independently within Japan. What causes problems: dependents working full-time without proper permission, the primary visa holder losing their job and not reporting it, or dependents staying in Japan while the primary holder lives abroad for extended periods.

If the primary visa holder's status is revoked or expires, dependent visas are also affected. Failing to report major changes like divorce or separation — part of family status reporting obligations — can result in dependent status being invalidated. Working beyond permitted hours is a violation that can lead to visa denial at renewal. If immigration suspects the dependent relationship is not genuine, such as a married couple living separately without explanation, they may investigate further. These situations require careful handling to avoid jeopardizing status.
