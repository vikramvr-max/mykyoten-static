Japan expects you to do things in a very specific sequence - doing them out of order wastes time.

Before arrival, focus only on visa eligibility and timing - you do not need a permanent address or bank account yet.

Moving to Japan Checklist: What to Do Before and After You Arrive

A practical checklist for moving to Japan. What to sort before you leave, what to do in your first 14 days, and the admin steps most people miss. Covers visas, city hall, health insurance, and more.

Most people don't fail at moving to Japan because the process is hard. They fail because they do everything in the wrong order. You'll see advice online about finding the perfect apartment, setting up a bank account, getting a phone plan, enrolling in health insurance, and registering at city hall. All of that is correct. The problem is Japan expects you to do these things in a very specific sequence, and if you try to skip ahead, you just get turned away and waste entire afternoons. This guide tells you what to do and when to do it. Nothing more.
