Japan Spouse Visa PR Fast Track 2026: 3yr/1yr to 5yr/3yr | MyKyoten

Spouses of Japanese nationals can apply for PR faster than the standard 10-year path. A draft guideline would lengthen the fast track from 3 years married…

What is commonly called a "spouse visa" in this context covers two formal statuses: Spouse or Child of Japanese National, and Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident. Family members on a Dependent visa, tied to someone else's work visa, do not have this fast track at all, and should start instead with the dependent visa document checklist or the work restrictions guide. Everyone else, see the standard PR eligibility guide.

Current rule: a substantive marital relationship (実体を伴った婚姻生活) continuing for 3 or more years, plus 1 or more years of continuous residence in Japan on the spouse status. Both conditions run in parallel and both must be satisfied.

Reported proposal, not yet in force: multiple outlets, including Asahi, Yomiuri, Kyodo, and Nikkei, reported in late July 2026 that ISA intends to lengthen this to 5 years of marriage and 3 years of residence, as part of a broader guideline revision that also covers the income and pension changes discussed in the PR eligibility guide. Public comment on that broader revision closed September 3, 2026. It has not been finalized. Reporting has suggested new substantive requirements would generally apply to applications from around April 2027, but ISA has not confirmed the exact transitional treatment. Check the official guideline page before relying on either figure for your own filing decision.

The 3-year requirement is based on the duration of a genuine, substantive marital relationship, not simply a mechanical count from the marriage certificate date. Time married before relocating to Japan can count toward it, but the relationship needs to be real and ongoing, not just legally registered.

The residence clock is separate and stricter: it counts only time actually, physically resident in Japan on the spouse status, starting from when that status began.

Example: a couple married for 3 years in their home country before relocating, and the foreign spouse has held spouse status in Japan for 14 months. Both conditions look satisfied on the numbers, marriage duration and residence duration, but ISA will still expect the relationship itself, not just the marriage date, to look genuine and continuous throughout.

Example: a couple who married after the foreign spouse was already resident in Japan on a different status, then changed to spouse status. The residence clock may already be well underway, but the marriage itself still needs to reach the required duration before the exception applies.

[warning box] If a marriage ends, through divorce or annulment, before your PR application is decided, you can no longer rely on the spouse-based exception for that application. Whether you can still qualify for PR depends on whether you meet another available route, for example the standard 10-year residence path, using the residence history you have already accumulated. This is not automatically a return to zero; it depends on your individual case.

Divorce also triggers its own, separate 14-day immigration reporting requirement tied to your underlying residence status, independent of anything about PR timing. If separation or divorce is part of your current situation, that reporting requirement is the more urgent issue and should be handled first.

The death of a Japanese spouse is treated differently from divorce for residence status purposes, but it does not restore a spouse-based PR calculation that was already interrupted before a decision. If either of these applies to your situation, this general guide is not detailed enough on its own; a licensed gyoseishoshi can assess your specific case.

If you married while already in Japan on a Dependent visa tied to a parent's or relative's work visa, the residence clock for the spouse fast track starts only from when your status change to spouse status was approved, not from your original arrival date as a dependent. Total years in Japan as a dependent do not count toward the 1-year (or proposed 3-year) residence requirement. Confirm your exact status-change approval date on your residence card before assuming an earlier start date.

What if I married my spouse overseas and we lived apart for a period before I moved to Japan? Marriage duration can still count from that period, provided the relationship was genuine and substantive throughout, not simply on paper. Long periods of separation without an ongoing relationship could be questioned; keep records (communication, visits, joint finances) that show continuity if your case involves extended time apart.

My spouse is a permanent resident, not a Japanese national. Does the same fast track apply? Yes, the same 3-year marriage and 1-year residence structure applies to spouses of permanent residents, not only spouses of Japanese nationals.

My 3-year period of stay is set to expire close to the reported April 2027 change. Does that affect me? This overlaps with the "longest period of stay" transition covered in the PR eligibility guide. The two changes, spouse-track duration and longest-period-of-stay, run on related but separate timelines; check both if your renewal falls in this window.

Does divorce after I've already filed, but before a decision, kill the application entirely? It removes the spouse-based ground you filed under. Whether the application can be reassessed under a different basis, or whether you would need to refile, is a case-specific question best handled with your immigration bureau or a licensed professional rather than assumed either way.

Does a long trip outside Japan break my 1-year residence count? There is no published day-count that automatically resets it. ISA weighs frequency, duration, and your re-entry permit status. Keep clear travel records if extended trips are part of your situation.

Does marriage time spent outside Japan count toward the 3-year requirement?

It can, provided the marital relationship was genuine and substantive throughout, not just registered. Residence duration is separate and only counts time actually resident in Japan on the spouse status.

Is the change from 3 years and 1 year to 5 years and 3 years final?

No. It remains a reported draft as of this writing. Public comment on the broader guideline revision closed September 3, 2026, and ISA has not published a finalized version or confirmed the exact transitional treatment.

If a marriage ends before PR is granted, does all PR eligibility reset to zero?

The spouse-based exception no longer applies to that application, but this does not automatically erase prior residence history. Whether another PR route, such as the standard path, remains available depends on the individual case.

Does a Dependent visa have any PR fast track?

No. The fast track applies specifically to Spouse or Child of Japanese National and Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident statuses. Dependent visa holders follow the standard path unless they change status.
