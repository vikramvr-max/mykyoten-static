My Number Card: What It Is and When You Need It | MyKyoten

Everything foreigners need to know about the My Number card in Japan. How to apply, what it is used for, and common mistakes.

My Number (the 12-digit number) is assigned automatically when you register your address - no application needed.

For most situations, the notification letter with your number is enough - you don't need the card immediately.

Get the card if you want online services (MyNa Portal / e-Tax), convenience-store certificate printing, or a government photo ID.
