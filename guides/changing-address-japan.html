How many days do you have to register an address change in Japan?

You must register your new address at your local city or ward office within 14 days of moving. This applies whether you are moving within the same city or to a different city entirely.

You need your residence card, passport, and proof of your new address such as a lease agreement or utility bill. The ward office will update the address sticker on the back of your residence card.
