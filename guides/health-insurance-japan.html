How health insurance works for foreigners in Japan. National Health Insurance enrollment, costs, and what is covered.

Health insurance is mandatory the moment you register your address - it's not optional.

You're automatically enrolled in National Health Insurance (NHI) unless your employer provides coverage.

Your enrollment date is your address registration date - you owe premiums from that day forward.

Delays don't exempt you - you'll receive backdated bills for months you weren't paying.

Travel insurance is not a substitute for mandatory Japanese health insurance.
