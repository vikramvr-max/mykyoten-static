Japan Airport Arrival Guide 2026: Immigration Steps & Residence Card

What actually happens when you arrive at a Japanese airport in 2026? Documents issued, mistakes to avoid, and next steps after landing.

The airport arrival in Japan isn't where your move gets decided. It's just where immigration confirms you're allowed to be here. Most people treat airport arrival like it's the final boss of moving to Japan. They stress about having perfect answers, a permanent address lined up, or a detailed life plan ready to present. None of that matters at the airport. The airport does exactly one thing: confirms your visa is valid and issues your residence card. That's it. Everything else happens later at city hall.
