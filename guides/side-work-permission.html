Is Side Work Allowed Without Permission? | MyKyoten

The rules around side jobs and freelance work for foreign residents in Japan on work visas.

Whether side work is allowed without permission depends entirely on your visa type and whether the activity falls within your approved scope.

Income amount doesn't determine whether permission is needed.

The few hours spent clarifying the rules now prevents serious problems later.

Submit an application at your regional immigration office. Processing takes approximately 2 weeks. You can also request it at the immigration counter when entering Japan on a dependent visa, where it is often granted on the spot.

Dependent visa holders with work permission are capped at 28 hours per week total across all jobs combined. This limit applies whether you have one part-time job or multiple. Exceeding it risks visa revocation at renewal.

Any paid activity outside your primary visa-permitted employment counts as side work. This includes freelancing, tutoring, content creation for pay, and part-time work at a second employer. Unpaid volunteer work is generally not subject to the restriction.

What happens if you work a side job without permission in Japan?

Working without permission is a visa violation that immigration can discover through tax records and employer filings. It can result in visa denial at renewal and in serious cases revocation of your current visa status.
